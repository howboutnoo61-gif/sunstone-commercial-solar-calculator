How the calculator works

  • The project price is treated as the net amount the installer must receive.
  • The gross loan is increased so the project price remains after the financing provider deducts its dealer fee.
  • Special-loan monthly payments assume the selected principal payment is made in month 18.
  • The estimated ITC is calculated from project price, while the required special payment is calculated from the larger gross loan.
  • Total paid includes scheduled monthly payments, the month-18 payment, and any maturity balloon.

Important limitations

The financing program requires a loan amount greater than $50,000. Smaller examples are illustrative only.

Rates and dealer fees are from the partner calculator as of August 6, 2026. They may change and are not a commitment to lend.

Tax credits depend on eligibility, tax basis, placed-in-service timing, and applicable law. This calculator is not tax, legal, or financial advice.
